## Requirement mapping

1. **One Fleet Supervisor side-effect leader** — implemented by `fleet_supervisor_control.py` using a dedicated CAS document composed with `execution-lease/v2`. Concurrent acquisition from the same revision has one winner; standby state carries no side-effect authority.
2. **One-shot Fleet effects** — semantic effect identity is deterministic SHA-256 over kind + target + intent + observed Fleet HEAD. Caller-selected alternate IDs are rejected. Claimed/submitted/unknown effects are retained and cannot be blindly replayed.
3. **Standby recovery** — prior leader effects can be reconciled only from exact provider observation bound to the original effect ID/intent. Running/unknown stays blocking; terminal/not-found can close the effect. Leader replacement still requires exact executor-stopped quiescence and never follows TTL alone.
4. **Real scheduler path fencing** — `fleet_watchdog_runtime.py` requires a live `GitFleetLeaderGuard` for positive effect budgets, pins one leader/Fleet HEAD per batch, persists leader/effect provenance, and rechecks before provider I/O. Read-only zero-effect assessment remains available to standby supervisors.
5. **Watchdog survivability fencing** — `watchdog_survivability_runtime.py` applies the same pinned leader binding to new create/enable/run/disable effects and rechecks immediately before provider I/O. A leader/Fleet-HEAD change before I/O is recorded blocked/not-submitted rather than as an unknown provider effect.
6. **Truthful liveness** — `execution_liveness.py` reports active only from exact fresh runtime-running evidence plus a fresh lease. Owner/TTL alone is unknown; stopped clean owner is orphaned_recoverable; stopped with pending effects is blocked_unknown_effects.
7. **Fail-closed final response** — `final_response_gate.py` requires an immutable revision-bound `execution-release-receipt/v1`, exact owner/generation/invocation/checkpoint binding and post-release continuity. `execution_lease_v2.release_cas` emits the receipt; `GitLeaseStore.read_revision` proves authoritative ancestry. A later generation cannot erase prior release proof.
8. **Atomic consumer adoption** — `consumer_adoption.py` requires detached complete preparation and exact package subtree before publication. `GitConsumerAdoptionPublisher` owns the transport boundary with a durable prepared→submitted→confirmed/unknown/rejected/aborted journal, normal non-force expected-head publication, exact readback and no replay after ambiguous outcome.
9. **Authority minimization** — the new assessments/gates explicitly do not grant project product-write, takeover, merge, release or scope expansion authority. Fleet leader authority remains limited to already-authorized control-plane effects.
