## Reporting and durable RCA

Keep historical command-timestamp v1 readable. Current substantive execution,
continuation, blocker and final reports use fresh runtime-clock evidence, unique
report identity and the same Russian `[HH:MM DD.MM]` prefix. A repeat command is
not grounds to suppress a later report. Reject missing/malformed/stale/future
stamps at the actual reporting boundary. Whole minutes refer only to completed
logical operations; unknown token observations are omitted, never synthesized.

Current final-response and terminal-bundle paths authenticate the invocation's
durable RCA inventory through its canonical coordination endpoint, with exact
original owned lease revision/owner/generation/invocation binding. A proposed
feedback payload or URL alone is not admission. Material entries need a durable
deduplicated admission or concrete deferral. Verified resolution additionally
needs pinned correction and regression verification receipts. Open/deferred
entries remain explicitly visible and do not block unrelated execution. The
pure historical evaluators must not turn missing current reporting authority
into a final-response permission. CLI closeout and managed finish must use this
binding, rather than merely returning an optional formatting helper.

This qualifies the package's supported emission/gate paths, not arbitrary host
chat text. Actual host adoption and personal acceptance remain separate.

## Detached and completed phases

Expose an explicit detached-preview validator: derive exact Git root/subtree,
net and full-history path sets and scope without demanding the assigned branch
already point at the candidate. The completed-result validator retains exact
assigned-branch and full-history proof after genuine worker adoption. A preview
does not confer completion, publication or launch authority. Runtime terminal
verification reads the original journal/request/receipt, binding boot and
process births and authentic descendant quiescence; wrapper exit alone is
insufficient. Unknown historical births stay unknown.
